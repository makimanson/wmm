WMM — RESPONSIBLE USE OF THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND RESOURCES

PREAMBLE: PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT

WMM (Wallpaper Multi-Monitor Manager) is an independent open-source project developed to provide tools for managing and customizing wallpapers in single-monitor and multi-monitor environments.
As part of its functionality, WMM integrates external services that allow users to search, browse, and obtain images and other resources available on the Internet.
This document outlines the principles governing these integrations, respect for the services involved, and the responsibilities associated with accessing third-party content.

1. WMM COMMITMENT

The integration of external resources into WMM aims to expand the application's capabilities while maintaining respect for those who make these resources available.
Responsible development goes beyond merely complying with a service's technical restrictions. It also involves avoiding unnecessary resource consumption, recognizing the work of others, and maintaining a respectful relationship with the communities and platforms that share their resources.
WMM adopts these principles as part of its development philosophy, seeking to ensure that new features are compatible with reasonable, conscious, and respectful use of external services.
This document is provided for informational purposes and expresses the project's general principles.
Under no circumstances does it replace the official terms of third-party services or modify the rights and obligations established by applicable licenses.

2. PRINCIPLES OF RESPONSIBLE USE

Access to external resources must be proportionate and must respect the technical limitations and conditions established by each service.
To this end, WMM adopts the following principles:

- Use the public interfaces and access mechanisms provided by each service.
- Respect published request limits and technical restrictions.
- Avoid unnecessary queries, repeated requests, and redundant downloads.
- Implement preventive measures to reduce unnecessary consumption of external resources.
- Respect error responses and service limitations without attempting to circumvent their restrictions.
- Favor moderate, interactive use over mass content acquisition.

These guidelines establish general development principles. Their technical implementation is adapted to the particular characteristics and conditions of each integration.
When WMM establishes additional voluntary limitations, these reflect responsible design decisions and must not be confused with obligations imposed by external services.

3. INDEPENDENCE AND RESPECT FOR EXTERNAL SERVICES

WMM does not claim ownership rights over the external services it integrates.
Their integration does not imply any commercial relationship, affiliation, sponsorship, official collaboration, participation in WMM's development, or endorsement by their operators.
Each service retains full authority over its infrastructure, content, access conditions, and usage policies.
WMM recognizes the work required to maintain these services and respects the conditions under which they are made available.

4. RIGHTS OVER THIRD-PARTY CONTENT

Resources obtained through external services may be protected by copyright, licenses, and specific conditions.
Their public availability or accessibility through an API does not, by itself, authorize unrestricted use, modification, or redistribution.
WMM does not claim rights over such content or grant permissions reserved for its legitimate rights holders.
WMM's GPLv3 license governs the software but does not automatically extend to resources obtained through external services.
References to the source service identify where content was obtained but do not, by themselves, establish authorship or usage rights.
Users are responsible for respecting the conditions applicable to each item of content.

5. PRIVACY AND EXTERNAL COMMUNICATIONS

Using online services involves communications with external servers that may include search terms, query parameters, network addresses, and other technical data.
The processing of this information is subject to each provider's privacy and data protection policies. WMM has no control over their infrastructure or data-processing practices.
Users are encouraged to consult the providers' official terms and policies to understand how they handle communications and the information received.

6. DOCUMENTATION AND APPLICABLE CONDITIONS

WMM provides informational documentation about its integrations, responsible use measures, and references to each service's official terms and policies.
This documentation is available under "About WMM > Licenses" and provides transparency regarding the operation and development of the integrations.
These conditions may change independently of WMM; the providers' official documents are the authoritative reference and take precedence over descriptions included in WMM.
Each service must be used in accordance with the terms currently in force.
