WALLHAVEN — INTEGRATION AND RESPONSIBLE USE IN WMM

1. ABOUT WALLHAVEN

Wallhaven (https://wallhaven.cc) is an online wallpaper platform that provides a public API allowing third-party applications to access certain resources from its catalog.

WMM (Wallpaper Multi-Monitor Manager) is an independent open-source project that uses the Wallhaven API to allow users to search, browse, and download wallpapers directly from the application.

This integration does not imply any official affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship, or partnership between WMM and Wallhaven.


2. USE OF WALLHAVEN RESOURCES

WMM uses the Wallhaven public API to perform searches and retrieve information about available wallpapers, including their properties, thumbnails, and download addresses.

Searches performed through WMM are restricted to content classified as SFW (Safe For Work), excluding sensitive or explicit content categories.

To respect Wallhaven's resources and infrastructure, WMM implements preventive measures for responsible usage:

- An internal limit of 40 API requests per minute, below Wallhaven's published limit of 45 requests per minute.
- A maximum of five original images per download batch.
- Sequential downloads, with a deliberate one-second delay between download attempts.
- Respect for service rate-limit responses, avoiding repeated requests when Wallhaven indicates that a limit has been reached.

These measures are voluntary WMM design decisions and should not be interpreted as additional restrictions imposed by Wallhaven.

WMM is designed for browsing wallpapers and manually downloading small selections, not for mass downloading or automated extraction of the Wallhaven catalog.


3. IMAGE ORIGIN AND COPYRIGHT

Wallpapers available through Wallhaven may be protected by copyright and subject to usage conditions established by their respective rights holders.

WMM does not claim ownership of these images or grant any rights over them. Its role is limited to facilitating access to resources made available through Wallhaven.

The GNU General Public License version 3, under which WMM is distributed, applies to the software and does not grant additional rights over images obtained from external services.

When a wallpaper is added through Wallpapers Online, WMM retains its source reference while the image remains registered in the local library. The application also provides a Website link to the corresponding information page on Wallhaven.

This reference identifies where the image was obtained. It does not constitute a declaration of authorship or determine the image's licensing conditions.

Users are responsible for respecting the copyright and applicable usage conditions of the images they download.


4. TERMS OF SERVICE AND PRIVACY

Access to Wallhaven resources through WMM is subject to the terms and policies established by Wallhaven.

Searches, thumbnail requests, and image downloads involve communication with Wallhaven's servers and may be subject to its privacy and data-processing policies.

WMM does not control Wallhaven's infrastructure or the way the service processes requests received from external applications.

Official information and current policies are available at:

Wallhaven website:
https://wallhaven.cc/

API documentation:
https://wallhaven.cc/help/api

Terms of Service:
https://wallhaven.cc/terms

Privacy Policy:
https://wallhaven.cc/privacy-policy

Wallhaven may modify its terms, policies, or technical restrictions independently of WMM.

The official documentation published by Wallhaven remains the authoritative reference and takes precedence over the informative descriptions contained in this document.


5. WMM COMMITMENT

The integration of Wallhaven into WMM aims to expand the application's capabilities while respecting the independence, resources, infrastructure, and rights of those who make the service possible.

Responsible resource usage is part of WMM's development principles and reflects its commitment to maintaining a respectful relationship with Wallhaven, its community, and the users of the application.

This document is provided for informational purposes only. It does not replace or modify Wallhaven's official terms and policies, nor does it alter the licenses or rights applicable to WMM or the images accessed through the service.
